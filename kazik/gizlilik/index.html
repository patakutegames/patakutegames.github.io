Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This policy applies to KAZIK, a mobile game developed by Pataküte Games.

  • KAZIK does not collect any personal data, and does not store or share it with anyone.
  • Your progress (money, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device, in the game's own local storage. It never leaves the device; uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • The game uses no internet connection. It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or any other third-party service, and needs no account.
  • The only permission the game uses is vibration (haptic feedback on hits); it can be turned off in the settings.
  • The game is suitable for players of all ages. No data is collected from anyone, including children.
  • Downloading the game through Google Play is subject to Google's own privacy policy; that process happens outside the game.
  • If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
